What a fractional new business director does

A fractional new business director (sometimes called a fractional growth lead or head of new business) gives an agency senior new business leadership part time. You get the experience of someone who has led over a hundred pitches, for the days you actually need.

In a typical month, that means:

  • Running the prospecting engine: target accounts, triggers worth acting on, and the value you send before a brief exists.
  • Coaching your team so outreach, meetings and pitches all follow one method.
  • Doctoring pitches: narrative, flow, design and rehearsal.
  • Keeping new business visible to leadership in the Permissionless Portal, so you see what is turning into pipeline.

Questions agencies ask me

How many days a month is a fractional new business director?

Whatever the work needs. We agree a set number of days a month up front, based on where you are and what you are going after, and review it as things change.

Will you replace our new business manager?

No. I work alongside the people you have, and give them a method, a platform and senior support. The aim is a team that runs it without me.

What is the difference between a fractional director and a consultant?

A consultant usually advises and hands over. A fractional director is part of the team for a set number of days each month, running new business with you and accountable for it.

Will you do the outreach for us?

No. Your team sends it and owns the relationships. I build the system, coach the people running it and provide the platform.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. I am based near Bath and most of the work happens remotely, with in-person sessions where they help. I work with agencies in the UK and further afield.
