Privacy, kept simple.

What happens to your details when you use this site, in plain English.

No trackingNo advertising or analytics cookies.
Only what you sendUsed to reply to you, nothing else.
Never soldYour details are never sold or shared for marketing.

Who we are

Scope & Lens is an agency growth consultancy run by Josh Fowler, based near Bath in the UK. We're responsible for the personal information you share through this site. Questions about it go to josh@scopeandlens.com.

What we collect

  • When you get in touch. Your name, agency, email address and anything you write in a form, whether that's an enquiry, an audit request, a Peers seat or a dashboard demo.
  • When you use the free tools. The answers you give are used to work out your result on the page. If you ask for your results by email, we receive your email address and your answers.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider logs things such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and working.

Why we use it

To reply to you, send you what you asked for, and, if we end up working together, to run that work. Our legal basis is your consent when you ask us for something, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries about our services. We won't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site only uses your browser's own storage for two small things:

  • Remembering that you've already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't replay on every page. This clears when you close the tab.
  • Remembering that you've dismissed the cookie notice.

Neither identifies you, and both stay on your device. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings, or show the cookie notice again.

Services that help run the site

A few trusted providers handle data on our behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Netlify hosts the site and receives the main enquiry form.
  • Formspree delivers the audit, Peers and dashboard demo forms to our inbox.
  • EmailJS sends healthcheck results when you ask for them by email.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve fonts and code to your browser, which means they see your IP address.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where that's the case, they use recognised safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it. Enquiries are kept while we're in conversation and for up to two years after our last contact, unless we're working together, in which case we keep what's needed for the work and our records.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. Just email josh@scopeandlens.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
