1What it means

What an agency new business consultant actually does

An agency new business consultant helps an agency win more of the right work, on purpose rather than by luck. With me, that comes down to three principles.

Only around 5% of B2B buyers are in market at any one time (Ehrenberg-Bass Institute for the LinkedIn B2B Institute). The work is making sure you are already front of mind with the other 95% when their brief lands.

  • Permissionless ProspectingChoosing the accounts worth winning, then being genuinely useful to them before a brief exists, over months rather than a three-email sequence.
  • Strategic StorytellingPitching the transformation you create, not a list of capabilities, so yours is the story they recall when they decide.
  • Visualised ValuePitches and credentials that are understood in seconds and easy to believe.
2Is it time?

Signs you need a new business consultant

  • You pitch plenty but convert too few, and you're not sure why.
  • Everyone prospects and pitches their own way, so nothing compounds.

How much does an agency new business consultant cost?

It depends on the shape of the work. A 30-Day Growth Sprint is a fixed project, a fractional retainer is a set number of days a month agreed up front, and Pitch and Win Together has no upfront fee. The discovery call is free, and you get a clear price after it.

Will you do the outreach for us?

No. I help your team build a permissionless prospecting system and provide the platform to run it. The relationships stay yours, and so does the system when I step back.

How quickly will we see results?

On a sprint the system is built in 30 days. Pipeline compounds over months rather than weeks: one agency went from zero to £2.5m in pipeline in nine months.

Do you work with network agencies, or only independents?

Both. Agencies of all shapes and sizes, independent and network, across creative, brand, media, digital, PR and integrated.

Do you work with agencies outside the UK?

Yes. I am based in the UK, near Bath, and work with agencies anywhere. Most of the work happens remotely, with in-person sessions where they help.
