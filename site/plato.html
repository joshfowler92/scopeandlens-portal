FAQ

Good questions.

What is Plato?

Plato is the new business platform for agencies, built by Scope & Lens. A crew of four, Nora, Gus, Priya and Otis, helps you pick the right brands, make something genuinely useful to give them, plan twelve weeks of touches and run it day to day, while Signals shows you who is leaning in.

Who is it for?

Agency founders, new business leads and growth teams who want a steady pipeline of the right clients, without cold pitching or buying lists. Creative, brand, digital, PR, media and specialist agencies all use the same method.

Does it send messages without me?

No. Nothing leaves without your say-so. You either send each message yourself, or approve it and let Amplify send it for you. You can switch at any time.

How is this different from lead-gen tools?

Lead-gen tools are built for volume: more sequences, more emails, more meetings of any kind. Plato is built for relevance. Fewer brands, a reason to talk now, something worth giving first and a pace that protects your reputation. It is the permissionless method Scope & Lens uses to win work for agencies, turned into software.

What does it cost?

Building your first campaign is free. Book a demo and Josh will walk you through running it with your team and what that looks like for an agency your size.

Do I need to change my CRM?

No. Plato tracks the conversations it starts and the revenue they turn into, and you can import the deals you already have.
