What does an agency growth consultant do?

Helps an agency win more of the right work. With me that means who to target, what to send them before a brief exists, how to pitch so the story sticks, and Plato, a platform that shows what is actually turning into pipeline.

What is a fractional growth lead for agencies?

Senior new business leadership without the full-time hire. I work inside your agency for a set number of days a month, running the prospecting engine, coaching the team and doctoring pitches.

What is permissionless prospecting?

Creating something genuinely useful for a prospect before they have asked for it, and keeping that value coming with relevant, persistent touchpoints over months rather than a three-email sequence. The first conversation starts from value, not a pitch, and you stay front of mind with the 95% who are not buying yet.

Who do you work with?

Agencies of all shapes and sizes, independent and network, across creative, brand, media, digital, PR and integrated. Usually where new business needs to be more predictable.

Do you work with agencies outside the UK?

Yes. I am based in the UK, near Bath, and happy to work with agencies anywhere. Most of the work happens remotely, with in-person sessions where they help.
