# Growth portal, version 11

- **Left-hand sidebar.** Home, Campaigns, then the four steps that build a campaign (Scout, Maker, Guide, Pilot) under the campaign you are building, then Results (Signals, Revenue), Team and Insights. Agency profile and settings sit at the foot. It shrinks to an icon rail on smaller laptops and a slide-out menu on phones.
- **Agents only where the work is built.** Who to target, What to share, How to reach and Launch have their agent. Home, Agency profile, Results, Team and Insights are plain.
- **New Home.** Your campaigns as cards (live or building, progress through the four steps, the numbers, and the next thing to do), a big New campaign button, the four agents as a relay, then who to contact today and the latest insights.
- **Campaigns.** Start a campaign from anywhere: pick the problem it solves, name it, and Scout takes it. Manage, rename, duplicate or delete them in Campaigns. The campaign you are building is always shown at the top of the four steps.
- **First login.** A short walkthrough, then Dot says hello and offers a full tour, or to build the first campaign together. The guide has eight steps, takes you to each one, shows you where to click and ticks each step off as it happens.
- **Problems.** The profile asks which problems your prospects have that you solve (up to five). Each campaign picks its problem in What to share, and the gift and every message are built around it.
- **Light, Steady and Full** are now properly different (around 6, 10 to 11, and 17 to 18 touchpoints over eight weeks), and no week ever has more than one email.
- **Events** are square tiles: real upcoming UK events (checked November 2026, linked to the organiser), searches for where each prospect's people are speaking, and hosting your own.
- **Launch is honest.** Only touches that are due can be ticked off, one at a time, in order. "I've sent it" only works once you have copied or opened the message. Later touches show the date they unlock. You can skip a touch that does not fit.
- **Logo.** Upload, replace or remove it in Agency profile or Settings.
